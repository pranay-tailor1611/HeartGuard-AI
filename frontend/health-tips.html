Cardiovascular Health & Wellness Guide

Evidence-based lifestyle guidelines, dietary rules, weekly fitness routines, and daily health checklists.

Heart Healthy Foods
  • Leafy Greens: Spinach, kale, and romaine rich in Vitamin K.
  • Whole Grains: Oats, brown rice, and quinoa high in soluble fiber.
  • Fatty Fish: Salmon and mackerel rich in Omega-3 fatty acids.
  • Nuts & Seeds: Walnuts, almonds, and flaxseeds for HDL cholesterol support.
Foods to Avoid
  • Processed Meats: Sausages and bacon high in sodium and preservatives.
  • Trans Fats: Deep-fried foods and commercial baked goods.
  • Refined Sugars: Sugar-sweetened beverages and candy.
  • High Sodium Foods: Canned soups and salty snacks (>2,000 mg/day).

Sleep & Hydration Protocol

Hydration Target: Consuming 2.5 - 3.0 L of water maintains optimal blood plasma volume and lowers vascular resistance.

Sleep Duration: 7-9 hours per night allows cardiac autonomic recovery, lowering resting blood pressure and heart rate.
