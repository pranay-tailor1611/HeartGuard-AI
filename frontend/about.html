Cardiovascular Disease Pathology Reference

Clinical reference on coronary artery disease, physiological risk factors, and diagnostic tools.

What is Heart Disease?

Cardiovascular Disease (CVD) encompasses structural and vascular disorders affecting cardiac muscle performance, including coronary artery disease (CAD), cardiac arrhythmias, heart valve defects, and vascular stiffening.

Clinical Symptoms
  • Chest Angina: Tightness, pressure, or squeezing in the chest.
  • Dyspnea: Shortness of breath during exertion or rest.
  • Fatigue & Dizziness: Lightheadedness or sudden exertion fatigue.
  • Palpitations: Rapid, fluttering, or pounding heart rate.
Biological Causes
  • Atherosclerosis: Lipid plaque buildup narrowing coronary arteries.
  • Endothelial Dysfunction: Chronic arterial wall inflammation.
  • Familial Factors: Genetic hypercholesterolemia.
  • Hypertension Stiffening: High vascular resistance damages arterial walls.
Key Risk Factors
  • Resting Blood Pressure > 130/80 mm Hg
  • Serum Cholesterol > 200 mg/dl
  • Fasting Blood Sugar > 120 mg/dl
  • Smoking and Tobacco Usage
  • Physical Inactivity & Obesity (BMI >= 30)
Diagnostic Tests
  • Resting 12-Lead Electrocardiogram (ECG)
  • Treadmill Exercise Stress Testing
  • Coronary Angiography (Fluoroscopy Vessels)
  • Echocardiogram Imaging
  • Fasting Lipid Profile Analysis
Medical Treatment

Includes antihypertensives, statins, antiplatelet agents, and surgical revascularization (stenting / CABG) when indicated.

Prevention

Adopting DASH or Mediterranean diet, maintaining 150 mins aerobic workout weekly, and avoiding tobacco products.

Emergency Warning Signs

Sudden crushing chest pain radiating to left arm or jaw, severe breathlessness, or cold sweats require emergency medical intervention (Call 108/112).
